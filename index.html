About Us

More Than A Gym

Dash Gym is South Bali's go-to fitness center, located in Pecatu, Kabupaten Badung. We offer fully equipped gym facilities, personal training, group fitness classes and flexible membership plans designed for locals, expats and Bali visitors alike.

Whether you're after serious weight training, a high-intensity cardio session or a guided HIIT class, Dash Gym gives you everything you need in a clean, welcoming environment — with a 1000sqm training floor, relaxation area and recovery access to help you go further.
